.. _examples_tpms:

Triply periodic minimal surfaces (TPMS)
---------------------------------------

Sheet and skeletal TPMS, density and cell-size grading, morphing between TPMS
families, cylindrical, spherical and swept coordinate frames, and infill of
arbitrary meshes.

Tetrahedral volume meshes made with the optional Meshers backend are
described in :ref:`meshers_backend`; their scripts are in
``examples/TPMS/meshers`` and are not part of this gallery.
